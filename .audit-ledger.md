## Phase 0 — Design system lock (anti-churn)

**Direction (decided with the owner, 2026-11-23):** keep the existing instrument-console philosophy (docs/19). That means Warm Carbon / Warm Parchment OKLCH neutrals, no accent hue, the four provenance frames, and hairlines. Add **a functional translucent material only on floating layers that sit over content**: command palette, peek drawer, menus/popovers and toasts. It is there so the board behind stays legible and context is kept. The page, sidebar, title bar and cards stay opaque. No re-hue and no re-skin.

Locked tokens (`apps/desktop/src/styles/tokens.css` is the single source):

- **Semantic colours (6):** `--text-1…4` (ink), `--bg-*` (surface ladder by lightness), `--border*` (hairlines), `--st-attention`, `--st-danger`, `--st-verified`, `--st-active`. Status colours are for glyphs and tints, never small text.
- **Type scale:** display 24/32, title 20/28, question 16/24, reading 14/22, body 13/20, meta 12/16, hint 11/16, code 12.5/20. Numbers use `--font-features-num` (tnum).
- **Space:** 4px grid, `--sp-1…10`.
- **Radii (concentric):** `--radius-xs 3` (kbd), `--radius-s 4` (chips), `--radius-m 6` (controls, cards), `--radius-l 10` (floating layers). The rule is **inner radius = outer radius − inset**. A floating layer (10) with a 4px inset holds 6px rows, and a 6px control with a 2px inset holds a 4px chip. New: `--float-inset: 4px`.
- **Material (floating layers only):** `--material-float` (theme-tuned translucent surface, thick: 0.86 Carbon / 0.84 Parchment), `--material-filter` (blur + saturate), `--material-edge` (hairline top light). Scrims `--scrim` / `--scrim-soft` and `--shadow-drawer` are per theme. The fallback is opaque `--bg-overlay` under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce` and wherever `backdrop-filter` is unsupported.
- **Motion:** transitions use transform/opacity only, between 100ms and 220ms (`--dur-fast/base/view/space`). Two stated folds animate space (`.decision-slot` grid rows, `.hunk-fold-inner` height, 160ms): an answered card's or accepted hunk's place closes, and space cannot collapse with a transform. The other exception is `--dur-attn` (700ms), the decay of the one-shot attention flash, which signals arrival and isn't a transition. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes motion.

The anti-churn rule: later rounds only fix deviations from this lock. They never change the palette or re-skin.
